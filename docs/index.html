Whether you’re a founder, product manager, team lead, or facilitator — this list helps you turn meetings from calendar clutter into a leveraged asset.

What Is Meeting Maestro?

Meeting Maestro is an AI-powered meeting assistant that:

  • Works from your existing email and calendar — just CC a smart assistant instead of doing admin yourself
  • Creates and sends calendar invites after you confirm the time over email
  • Adds titles, locations/links, and reminders automatically
  • Can plug into your existing stack over time (Zoom/Meet, CRMs, notes, automation tools)

This repository is a complementary resource: a curated guide to the broader “meeting stack” — everything that pairs well with Meeting Maestro’s assistant model.

Scheduling & Calendar Tools

Tools that make it easier to find a time, share availability, and avoid back-and-forth emails. Many of these pair well with a CC-based assistant like Meeting Maestro.

  • Cal.com — Open-source scheduling platform with powerful routing, round-robin, and team booking. cal.com
  • Calendly — Popular meeting scheduling tool for sales, recruiting, and customer success. calendly.com
  • Google Calendar — Calendar backbone for many teams; integrates with almost every meeting stack. calendar.google.com
  • Microsoft Outlook / 365 Calendar — Enterprise calendar standard with deep integration into Microsoft 365.
  • Cron (by Notion) — Opinionated calendar app for power users, with keyboard-first scheduling. cron.com

Video Meeting Platforms

Core tools for running remote or hybrid meetings. Most of these are where Meeting Maestro–scheduled invites will land.

  • Zoom — Standard for many remote teams and webinars.
  • Google Meet — Simple, browser-based meetings integrated with Google Workspace.
  • Microsoft Teams — Enterprise chat + meetings; deep 365 integration.
  • Whereby — Lightweight video meetings with persistent rooms.
  • Around — Video meetings designed for collaboration and low-fatigue vibes.

AI Meeting Assistants & Note-Takers

Record, transcribe, summarize, and action your meetings automatically. Great companions to scheduling-focused tools.

  • Fathom — Free meeting recorder & AI-powered summaries. fathom.video
  • tl;dv — AI note-taker for Zoom, Meet, and more, with highlight clips. tldv.io
  • Fireflies.ai — Voice assistant that records, transcribes, and indexes meetings.
  • Grain — Capture and repurpose meeting insights into snippets and notes.
  • Otter.ai — Real-time transcription and collaborative notes for meetings.

Meeting Agendas & Templates

Templates and tools for structuring meetings so they’re focused, time-bound, and outcome-driven.

  • Notion — Rich docs for agendas, recurring meeting templates, and action logs.
  • Coda — Meeting hubs with buttons, automations, and integrated tasks.
  • Fellow.app — Purpose-built meeting agenda and action item tool.
  • Loom + Docs — Async video + a simple doc for lightweight agenda and recap flows.
  • Meeting Maestro Agenda Patterns (coming soon) — A set of email-first agenda templates that work with CC-based assistants.

Facilitation Frameworks & Practices

Concepts, frameworks, and resources for running better standups, retros, 1:1s, strategy sessions, and workshops.

  • Agile Retrospectives — Classic “What went well / What didn’t / Actions” model for team learning.
  • Lean Coffee — Structured but agenda-less meeting format; participants shape the topics live.
  • Shape Up Cycles — Pitch reviews and bet meetings from Basecamp’s Shape Up method.
  • 1:1 Frameworks — Recurring check-in templates for managers and direct reports (e.g. “Spotify 1:1,” “Career, Care, Context” formats).
  • Workshop Playbooks — Miro / MURAL template libraries for ideation, roadmapping, and product strategy sessions.

Async & No-Meeting Alternatives

Meetings aren’t always the answer. These tools help you ship updates, decisions, and discussions without a live call.

  • Loom — Async video updates instead of status meetings.
  • Slack / Teams Channels — Structured channel rituals (e.g. #daily-standup) as meeting replacements.
  • Twist — Async-first team communication as an alternative to chat + meetings.
  • Range — Daily check-ins and weekly async updates.
  • Notion / Coda Update Docs — Long-form async decision records & memos.

Meeting Automation Workflows

Example workflows using tools like n8n, Make, Zapier, and Meeting Maestro’s email-first model to reduce manual admin around meetings.

Common Automation Patterns

  • 📩 “CC the assistant” → create calendar event after a time is agreed in email.
  • 🧾 Auto-generate meeting notes docs in Notion or Google Docs when an event is created.
  • ✅ Sync action items from meeting notes into tools like Linear, Jira, ClickUp, or Asana.
  • 🔔 Slack reminders before key recurring meetings (e.g. retro, planning, leadership sync).
  • 📊 Meeting analytics (frequency, attendees, topics) for leadership visibility.

Example Tools for Automation

  • n8n — Self-hostable workflow automation for calendars, email, Slack, docs, and CRMs.
  • Make (Integromat) — Visual automation builder for meeting workflows.
  • Zapier — Connects calendars, CRMs, video tools, docs, and more.
  • Meeting Maestro — Email-triggered assistant; workflows start simply by CC’ing an address.

Developer APIs for Meetings & Calendars

For engineers building their own meeting, scheduling, or assistant products — these APIs make it easier to work with calendars, video tools, and communication platforms.

  • Google Calendar API — REST API for events, calendars, and availability.
  • Microsoft Graph Calendar API — Access Outlook / 365 calendars programmatically.
  • Zoom API — Create meetings, manage users, and pull recordings.
  • Google Meet via Google Workspace APIs — Use Calendar + Meet integrations to manage meeting links.
  • Slack / Teams APIs — Send reminders, summaries, and updates to channels.
  • Cal.com APIs — Deep integration for scheduling experiences, webhooks, and routing logic.
